# First review

- Surface: Product, version, and platform tested
- Date: When you tested it
- Evidence: Links, examples, or reproducible steps
- Verdict: Pending

## What I tried

Describe a real task, the setup, and what a useful result would have been.

## What happened

Record what you observed. Keep guesses and product claims separate
from results you reproduced yourself.

## Friction

Explain what broke, what worked around it, and what remains untested.

## Verdict

Write your conclusion after testing. Before publishing, replace the
placeholders, set `status: published`, and add the actual publication
timestamp as `published_at: YYYY-MM-DDTHH:MM:SSZ`.
