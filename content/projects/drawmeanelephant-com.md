# draw me an elephant

The origin story, and where the handle comes from: a long-running art
and letter-writing adventure. Handwritten letters sent to breweries
around the world asking one simple question — "Will you draw me an
elephant?" — with the stories, artwork, and history preserved, rebuilt
as a Boris-powered static site. Per the repo's own description.

Everything else on [[projects/index]] is downstream of a letter and a
question. That is the kind of fact that does not get said in plain
words often enough, so: it is said here now.
