# Virelai Sans

The house typeface: a geometric neo-grotesque, plus the Sexiburger
mark. Named for the fixed-form poem, kin to [[projects/virelai-os]].

Status: active. Receipts:
[virelai-sans on GitHub](https://github.com/drawmeanelephant/virelai-sans).

## Small company

Three project marks, two ways: the approved SVG artwork on the left;
live color-font glyphs on the right, with SVG fallbacks if the fonts
cannot load. These are accents. Geist still does the reading here.

Oliver is `U+E008` and the Filed robot is `U+E009` in **Virelai
Mascots Regular**. Sexiburger represents VirelaiOS and remains
`U+E001` in **Virelai Sans Regular**, not the companion mascot face.
The webfonts preserve the original outlines and COLR/CPAL color
layers. The SVGs come from the same approved artwork.
