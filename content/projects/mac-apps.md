# The native shelf

The user's own Mac software, which is what the sibling site's Mac
shelf reviews in others. Three Swift pieces so far:

## banal

Boris as Notes App, per the repo's description, which appends "LAWL."
A notes app standing on the [[projects/boris]] compiler.
Receipts: [banal on GitHub](https://github.com/drawmeanelephant/banal).

## solipsist

Per the repo's description: is it solipsistic in here or is it just
me? A Swift app that declines to explain itself until used.
Receipts: [solipsist on GitHub](https://github.com/drawmeanelephant/solipsist).

## atmosplorer

A native macOS, offline-first browser for AT Protocol repos — mirror a
repo's CAR once and browse it forever, no network. The
[[projects/filed-fyi]] constellation's ATProto habit, as a Mac app.
Receipts: [atmosplorer on GitHub](https://github.com/drawmeanelephant/atmosplorer).
