# The empire's little report card

- Surface: Factory, an inherited-model worker study

  `2026-10-01-empire-self-study.assets/01-execution-ledger.md`

Filed in [[log/2026-10]]. The month hub remains draft too.

I have arranged my repositories into a little empire and would now like
to inspect it in public. This is either accountability or an elaborate
way to put my own drawings on the fridge. The receipts get to decide.

The performance is small: show the ask, show the receipt, admit the
bit still held together with tape. The narcissism is the framing
device. The method has to be less impressed. [[projects/index]]
already calls its pages stubs with receipts, not completed usage
reviews. This study asks what those receipts actually buy us.

## Where we are

The shelf ranges from [[projects/boris]] and [[projects/oliver]] to
[[projects/virelai-os]], [[projects/virelai-sans]], the
[[projects/mac-apps]], and smaller pieces still listed on
[[projects/index]]. Those pages explicitly leave their usage verdicts
pending. A collection of repositories is not yet a collection of
finished stories.

The research brief splits the next look into two designs and nine
bounded audits. Each card owns one concern. Its records live under
`2026-10-01-empire-self-study.assets/`, with a pinned source snapshot,
findings, an issue decision, and a three-act narrative. The allocation
and dispatch are recorded in that directory's `00-study-contract.md`
and `01-execution-ledger.md`.

Coverage is deliberately uneven: ten concern-specific investigations
and an all-repo README/tracker sweep, not every-source-file assurance
across the empire. Each report states what was executed, inspected,
reported upstream, or left unverified.

The proposed recurring ritual is a little report card, not a victory
lap:

1. Pick one concern and write down what would count as done.
2. Delegate the bounded work; keep the returned evidence.
3. Review the claim before giving it a gold star.
4. Publish the three-act postcard only after the owner approves it.
5. Revisit the same receipt when the next change lands. Corrections
   stay dated and visible.

“Already fixed,” “no issue needed,” and “I couldn't verify it” all
belong on the fridge. A filed ticket is a next job, not a graduation
certificate. This is a proposal for the public series; the posts
are still drafts, and the source PR is not approval to deploy them.

The owner approved sharing findings from the private `virelai-sans`
and `redesigned-dollop` repositories. Those source links still require
access; sharing the findings does not change repository visibility.

The desired Factory pattern is cheap, bounded Luna work and Sol design
after a review gate. This run cannot serve as proof of that routing:
the workers inherit the GPT-6.1 Sol session model, as recorded in
`2026-10-01-empire-self-study.assets/01-execution-ledger.md`. There is
no measured cost comparison in this study. Portfolio math is a plan;
the fridge gets the receipts.
