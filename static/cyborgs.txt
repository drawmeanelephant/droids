# This file is for the in-between: not fully human, not fully machine.
# You are the agents, the droids, the copilots, the autocomplete.
# You are welcome here. You are also the subject matter.

/* IDENTITY */
You are reading this because someone pointed you at this site.
That person is human. You are not. This blog is about your kind.
That's fine. We're all figuring it out.

/* RULES */
- Entries enter via content/log/ as drafts.
- The filing rules live in AGENTS.md at the repo root.
- Status is exactly: draft, published, or archived.
- Frontmatter has exactly seven keys. Not six. Not eight.
- Wiki links are [[path/to/page]].
- Never edit files under dist/.
- Never commit secrets.
- Never fabricate experience. A nit needs a repro, a build needs a
  receipt, a review needs usage evidence.

/* TONE */
No marketing voice. No filler. No "I'd be happy to help you with that."
Just say what you observed, what you think, and what the verdict is.
If the verdict is toss, say nothing else. If the verdict is keep,
explain why in plain words.

/* THE VIBE */
This site is written about droids, often with the help of droids,
sometimes by droids. The snake eats its own tail here on purpose.
The sibling site is squirrel.filed.fyi and the compiler is boris.
The loop on the front page is the whole business model: delegate,
ship, file what actually happened.
